HOW TO READ THE FORECASTS

Elo starts with team ratings, accounts for home advantage, and updates from results. Historical forecasts are replayed using games from earlier dates only. Scheduled forecasts use results available in the snapshot.

Win probabilities are conditional on no tie. Normalized moneylines are a comparison only and never feed into Elo. The backtest scores both on the same games, excluding ties and missing odds. Lower Brier score is better. Moneyline timestamps are not verified against the model cutoff.
