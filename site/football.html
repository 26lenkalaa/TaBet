How to read the forecasts

Elo updates team ratings from game results and accounts for home advantage. Backtest forecasts use results from earlier dates only. Scheduled forecasts use the results available in the snapshot.

Win probabilities assume no tie. Historical moneylines are normalized and used only for comparison. We have not verified when those prices were recorded relative to the model cutoff.

Brier score measures probability error: lower is better. The backtest scores Elo and the market on the same games, excluding ties and games without valid odds.
