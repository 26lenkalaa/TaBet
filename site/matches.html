What the labels mean

Backtest Rebuilt afterwards, but using only information available before that match: results, xG and, for V2x, the confirmed lineups. This is how every past season was scored.

Logged A real prediction, saved 24–32 hours before kickoff and never changed. p0 uses no lineups; pE uses the lineups predicted by Fantasy Football Scout and RotoWire.

Preview Today's lineup-free forecast for an upcoming match. It updates as results come in, until the logged forecast replaces it.

Market Bookmakers' average odds turned into probabilities, with their margin removed. Earlier odds are from Friday or Tuesday afternoon; closing odds are from kickoff. Odds are only ever a yardstick, never a model input.

Log-loss scores the probability given to what happened: lower is better. A confident miss costs the most.
