WHAT THE LABELS MEAN

Backtest Rebuilt afterwards, but using only information available before that match: results, xG and, for V2x, the confirmed lineups. This is how every past season was scored.

Logged A real prediction, saved 24–32 hours before kickoff and never changed. p0 uses no lineups; pE uses the lineups predicted by Fantasy Football Scout and RotoWire.

Preview Today's forecast for an upcoming match, without lineups and, once predicted XIs are published, with them. It updates until the logged forecast replaces it.

Market Bookmakers' average odds turned into probabilities, with their margin removed. "Market at our forecast time" was saved in the same run as TaBet's logged forecast; earlier odds are from Friday or Tuesday afternoon; closing odds are from kickoff; latest odds are the newest snapshot. Odds are only ever a yardstick, never a model input.

Log-loss scores the probability given to what happened: lower is better. A confident miss costs the most.
